> You can list all available resource names and their shortcuts directly in your cluster by running kubectl api-resources

Common Core Resources
-  po for pods
-  svc for services
-  deploy for deployments
-  cm for configmaps
-  sec for secrets
-  ns for namespaces
-  no for nodes

Workloads & Controllers
-  rs for replicasets
-  rc for replicationcontrollers
-  sts for statefulsets
-  ds for daemonsets
-  job for jobs
-  cj for cronjobs

Storage & Configuration
-  pv for persistentvolumes
-  pvc for persistentvolumeclaims
-  sc for storageclasses
-  ing for ingresses
-  ep for endpoints

Access & Security
-  sa for serviceaccounts
-  hpa for horizontalpodautoscalers
-  csr for certificatesigningrequests
